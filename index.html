Investment Guide

Capital · Allocation · Growth

Forex is the global market where currencies are exchanged. It is the largest and most liquid market in the world, open 24 hours a day, 5 days a week. Prices move because of interest rates, economic data, geopolitics and supply-demand — not because of "signals".

Never invest money you need for rent, EMI, education or emergencies. A safe rule:

  • Emergency fund of 6 months — untouched
  • Zero high-interest debt
  • Only 5–15% of net savings allocated to trading
  • Money you can lose without changing your lifestyle

Risk a maximum of 1% of your account per trade. With a $10,000 account that's $100 risk per trade. This means even 10 consecutive losses only costs you about 9.6% of your account — completely recoverable. Traders who risk 10% per trade blow up after 10 losses.

Anyone promising 50% monthly is selling you a dream. Realistic benchmarks:

  • Beginner: 0–3% per month (after learning phase)
  • Consistent trader: 3–8% per month
  • Professional funded trader: 5–10% per month with strict risk
  • Anything above 20% monthly long-term is extremely rare

Compounding is the real wealth engine. $1,000 at 5% monthly becomes about $1,795 in 12 months, and $3,225 in 24 months — without adding a single rupee. Decide early: reinvest everything for growth, or withdraw a fixed percentage monthly for income. Never withdraw your risk capital.

Forex trading income is taxable in India. Maintain a clean record of every trade, deposit and withdrawal. Consult a qualified chartered accountant for your specific situation — I am a trader, not a tax advisor.

Trading Guide

Structure · Execution · Discipline

Before indicators, learn structure: higher highs, higher lows, lower highs, lower lows. Trend, range, or transition — every chart is in one of these three states. Trade with structure, not against it.

Never enter without all 6 boxes ticked:

  • Is the higher timeframe trend clear?
  • Is there a valid entry trigger?
  • Is the stop loss placed at a logical level?
  • Is the risk:reward at least 1:2?
  • Does the position size match my 1% rule?
  • Am I emotionally neutral right now?

London (12:30–4:30 PM IST) — highest volatility, best for EUR, GBP.
New York (6:00–10:30 PM IST) — strong USD moves, overlaps with London.
Asian (5:30–11:30 AM IST) — quieter, range-bound, good for JPY, AUD.
Avoid the 30 minutes around major news releases unless you trade news deliberately.

Every trade gets a stop loss before entry. Never move a stop loss further away to "give it room" — that is how a small loss becomes an account-ending loss. The stop loss is the cost of doing business; it is not a failure.

Log every trade with a screenshot, the reason for entry, the emotion you felt, and the outcome. After 50 trades, patterns appear — usually the same 2–3 mistakes repeated. Fixing those mistakes is worth more than any new indicator.

Trading is 20% strategy and 80% behaviour. After two losses, step away for 30 minutes. After a big win, do not increase lot size. Stick to a maximum of 2–3 trades per day. Boredom trades and revenge trades destroy more accounts than bad analysis ever will.
